Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-06-16 · Lapp's Online Dynamics LLC

This Privacy Policy describes how VolleyHub, operated by Lapp's Online Dynamics LLC, collects, uses, and protects your information. VolleyHub is a local volleyball tournament management app for Lancaster County, Pennsylvania.

Information We Collect

  • Phone number — used to authenticate your account via a one-time passcode (OTP) sent by SMS.
  • Display name — shown to organizers and other participants within the app.
  • Team and tournament registration data — the tournaments you create or register for and your registration status.

How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate your account using SMS one-time passcodes.
  • To send organizer approval notifications when your organizer request is approved.
  • To coordinate tournaments, including registration status updates.

SMS Consent & Mobile Information

No mobile information — including your phone number — will be shared with or sold to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text-messaging opt-in consent is never shared with any third party. SMS messages are sent only through our messaging provider (Twilio) solely to deliver the transactional one-time-passcode and account messages described above. Messaging is transactional only — we do not send marketing messages. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help. Message and data rates may apply.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information, and we never share your information with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes. Information is shared only with subcontractors and service providers strictly necessary to operate the app (for example, Twilio for SMS delivery and Supabase for secure data storage), and only as needed to provide the service; these providers are contractually prohibited from using your information for their own purposes.

Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your information for as long as your account is active. To request deletion of your data, contact us at sammy@lappsonlinedynamics.com and we will delete your personal information.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact sammy@lappsonlinedynamics.com.
