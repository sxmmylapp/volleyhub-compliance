About VolleyHub

VolleyHub is a local volleyball tournament management app for Lancaster County, Pennsylvania. It lets tournament organizers publish upcoming tournaments and lets players register their teams. The app coordinates account access, organizer approvals, and registration logistics; it is not a payment processor — any money moves directly between organizers and players outside the app.

How SMS Opt-In Works

All SMS messaging is strictly transactional and is initiated by the end user. A user opts in to receive SMS during sign-in by completing the steps below on the VolleyHub sign-in screen:

  1. The user opens the VolleyHub app and is taken to the Sign in screen.
  2. The user enters their own mobile phone number in the phone number field.
  3. Directly beneath the “Send code” button, the user reads the SMS consent disclosure (shown verbatim below) explaining that tapping the button consents to receiving a one-time passcode by SMS.
  4. The user taps “Send code,” which constitutes their opt-in, and VolleyHub sends a one-time passcode (OTP) by SMS to the number they entered.
  5. The user enters the passcode in the app to verify their phone and sign in.

VolleyHub sign-in screen — phone field, “Send code” button, and the consent disclosure shown at the opt-in point.

Exact consent language shown in the app

The following text is displayed in-app immediately below the “Send code” button:

“By tapping Send code, you agree to receive a one-time passcode SMS from VolleyHub at the number provided. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.”

Types of Messages We Send

  • One-time login passcodes — a numeric code to verify the user's phone number and sign in.
  • Organizer approval notifications — a one-time notice when a user's request to become a tournament organizer is approved.
  • Tournament registration status updates — notices when a team's tournament registration status changes.

Message Frequency

Messages are transactional only — there is no marketing or promotional messaging. Message frequency varies by account activity (for example, each sign-in attempt sends one passcode). Most users receive only a small number of messages.
