Delete your account

Effective date: 2026-11-11 · Lapp's Online Dynamics LLC

This page explains how to delete your VolleyHub account, including when you no longer have the app. VolleyHub is published by Lapp's Online Dynamics, the developer name on Google Play and the App Store, and is operated by Lapp's Online Dynamics LLC.

The fastest way: in the app

Open VolleyHub, go to Settings → Account → Delete account, then confirm. Your account is deleted right away.

Without the app: email us

Email sammy@lappsonlinedynamics.com from any email address, with the subject “VolleyHub account deletion request”. Tell us the phone number your account signs in with.

Before we delete anything, we confirm the number is yours. We reply to set a time with you. At that time we text a one-time code to that number, and you read it back to us. The code expires about a minute after it is sent, so the read-back happens live: on a call, or in a reply you send within that minute.

Once the number is confirmed, we delete the account within 30 days. We usually do it the same day, and we reply to tell you it is done. If the number never had a VolleyHub account, there is nothing to delete and we tell you so.

What is deleted

  • Your phone number and your display name. Both are removed from your account record and replaced with placeholders, and your phone number can no longer be used to sign in to that account.
  • Your sign-in sessions. Every device signed in to the account is signed out.
  • Your push notification registrations. No device receives VolleyHub notifications for the account.
  • Your places on league teams you joined as a player, along with the phone number the captain entered for you on those teams.
  • The payment receipt name on any team registration you made as captain.
  • Your request for organizer access, if you made one: the copy stored with your request (the name, contact email and message you typed into it) is deleted, and no email about that request is sent after your account is deleted.

What is kept, and why

  • Tournaments and leagues you organized, and team registrations you made as captain, stay so that other players' records stay whole. Your name is not shown on them; they show “Deleted user” instead.
  • If you organized events, the Cash App name ($cashtag) you set for players to pay you stays on the tournaments and leagues you organized, and stays public there, after your account is deleted.
  • A draft league you deleted is kept as a recoverable copy for 30 days from the day you deleted it, then removed automatically. The copy includes everything typed into it, such as its name, location and description. Deleting your account does not remove that copy sooner.
  • Phone numbers you entered as a captain for players who never joined your team stay on that team's record until the team record is deleted or we are asked to delete them. If your own number was entered by a captain and you want it removed, email us and we will delete it.
  • A service-only record that a team was sent an “added you” notification for a phone number, which stops repeat notifications, is kept for 30 days after the last such notification, then deleted automatically.
  • If you asked for organizer access, each email that told us about your request stays; each holds the name on your request, your account name, your phone number, your user id, when you asked, the request text and the contact email you gave, if any. The copies in the emails we already received are not part of your account and are not deleted by that.
  • Reports you submitted about other users' content stay, so our moderation decisions keep their record.
  • Sign-in attempts for your number are kept for 7 days to stop abuse, then deleted automatically.

Deletion is permanent and can't be undone. Our Privacy Policy describes everything we collect and how long we keep it.
