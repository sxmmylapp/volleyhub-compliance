Support

VolleyHub is a local volleyball tournament management app operated by Lapp's Online Dynamics LLC. Need help with your account, a tournament, or a registration? We're happy to help.

Contact Us

Email sammy@lappsonlinedynamics.com and we'll get back to you as soon as we can. Please include the phone number you sign in with so we can find your account.

Common Questions

  • Sign-in trouble? VolleyHub signs you in with your phone number and a one-time passcode sent by SMS. If a code doesn't arrive, wait a minute and request a new one. Message and data rates may apply.
  • Payments — VolleyHub is not a payment processor. Entry fees move directly between players and organizers through Cash App; the app only coordinates who owes what and when. Payment questions about a specific tournament are best directed to its organizer.
  • Delete your account or data? Email us at the address above and we'll delete your personal information.
